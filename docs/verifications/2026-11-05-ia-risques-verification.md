## Uncertain or flagged
1. **Grace et al.** is a preprint (v3, Oct. 2025), not peer-reviewed as far as I found. It had a 15 % response rate.
2. **Project Panama quotes** come from the Washington Post via relays (not independent). I could not open the WaPo article or the unsealed exhibit.
3. **US "no" vote on A/RES/80/57** is from un-project.org and Opinio Juris, not the official UN roll call.
4. **Lavender 37,000** is an allegation from anonymous sources, and the IDF disputes it. The slide attributes it ("Selon +972").
5. **Anthropic vs. Pentagon** is ongoing litigation (N.D. Cal. injunction versus D.C. Circuit). The status may have changed after Sept. 2026.
6. **LeCun "foutaise"** is a translation of "complete B.S." (WSJ is paywalled; quote relayed by several outlets).
7. **Settlement per-work amount**: "environ 3 000 $ US" is gross. Net is about 2,930 $ before interest. First payments are only expected by 15 Nov. 2026.
8. "Des millions" (détruits) is not a precise count because none is public.
